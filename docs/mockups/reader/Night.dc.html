Reading without that pause is just scrolling with better manners. You finish the page, feel briefly clever, and a week later cannot say what it argued.

So the system is deliberately small: read, mark the line that earns it, say it back in a sentence of your own.

Over time the cards stop being a record of what you read and become a record of what you thought. That is the part no feed can give you.
