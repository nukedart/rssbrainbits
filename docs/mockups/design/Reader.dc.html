The notecard habit that outlasts every app

Every book I have written began as a stack of index cards. Each card held a single idea, copied from something I had read and phrased in my own words, filed under a theme I could find again a year later.

The tool never mattered. What mattered was the small act of stopping to decide what was worth keeping.

Reading without that pause is just scrolling with better manners.
